FAQ

Questions businesses often ask.

Do I need a completely new website?

Not necessarily. We start by checking what you already have. If your existing website can be repaired, improved or modernised, we'll tell you. A rebuild is recommended only when it makes sense.

What does the free Website Health Check look for?

The scanner checks important website health signals including SEO, performance, mobile usability, accessibility, technical issues and customer conversion opportunities. The results are presented in a clear report explaining what was found and what could be improved.

Is the Website Health Check really free?

Yes. The initial Website Health Check is designed to give you a practical overview of your website's current health and highlight areas that may benefit from improvement. There is no obligation to purchase a service.

Can you work on a website someone else built?

Yes. We can inspect an existing website and work with the available code or platform where practical. We'll first assess what is already there before recommending the best approach.

What kinds of website problems can you fix?

Depending on the website, we can help with issues such as mobile usability, technical SEO, broken or inaccessible elements, performance improvements, customer contact options, layout problems and general website updates.

How much does website rescue cost?

Small website fixes start from R1,500. Larger improvement projects receive a clear quote based on the actual work required after reviewing the website and the recommended improvements.

Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?

Yes. Site Rescue Studio is designed for remote clients and can work with businesses internationally.

What happens after my website is fixed?

You can manage the website yourself or choose an ongoing Website Care plan for maintenance, updates, monitoring and future improvements.

What happens after I receive my Website Health Report?

You can use the report to make improvements yourself, or contact us to discuss the findings. If you would like help, we'll focus on the issues that are most relevant to your website and business rather than recommending unnecessary work.
