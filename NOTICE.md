# Overture Gauteng Places feed

This feed is derived from the Overture Maps Places theme. It contains
business names, public website URLs, city/region, category, confidence,
GERS feature IDs, and per-record source/license provenance. It does not
contain phone numbers or email addresses.

Records are included only when their source provenance is explicitly
licensed under CDLA-Permissive-2.0 or CC0-1.0. Any record with a
Foursquare source, a structured brand association, a known national
chain/franchise name or domain, or a store-locator URL is excluded.
The underlying source datasets and
licences are retained in each record's locationEvidence.sources.

- Overture Maps attribution: https://overturemaps.org/
- CDLA-Permissive-2.0: https://cdla.dev/permissive-2-0/
- CC0-1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This is a candidate feed, not a verified prospect list. Consumers must
deduplicate against their own records and perform DNS, live homepage,
parked-domain, identity and geography checks before use.
